## Ideas and inspiration

The tools are teaching instruments about techniques, movements and ideas. Where a person, studio or movement is named it is a short, factual "inspired by" credit only. Nothing here is made, endorsed or approved by them, and no recordings, artwork, lyrics or card texts by anyone else are included.

The prompt cards are our own wording, inspired by the Oblique Strategies deck (1975). No card text from that deck is reproduced.

## Quotations

Only texts that are in the public domain in the UK (author died in 1955 or earlier) are quoted, marked "public domain; our translation". Everything else is our own paraphrase. See `RIGHTS-LOG.md` for every change made in the November 2026 rights pass.
